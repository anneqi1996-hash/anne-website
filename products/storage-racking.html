How this group is quoted and delivered

Same production base, same packing discipline. What changes between categories is the specification sheet, not the way we work.

Specify from a drawing or a room

Send a drawing, a sample photo or the room dimensions. We return a dimensioned proposal with material, thickness and finish called out — not a generic catalogue page.

Sample before tooling

A pre-production sample is built and shipped for approval. Powder colour, hardware and packing are locked only after you sign it off.

Load the container once

Flat-pack cartons, moisture barrier, and a loading plan issued before packing so volume and weight are agreed up front. Mixed categories can share a container.
