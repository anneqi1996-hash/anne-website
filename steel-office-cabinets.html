Steel Office Cabinets for Fit-Out and OEM Programmes

Swing, sliding and glass-door cabinets with internal shelving or a hanging rail, finished to match the file cabinets on the same floor.

  • Door matrix covers open storage, closed storage and display in one body depth.
  • Interior configured per floor: shelves, hanging rail, or a mix.
  • Same colour and texture as the file cabinet range, so a fit-out reads as one system.

Customisation

What can be changed

Anything below is quoted as a variant of a listed model. Send the combination and we price it against the base SKU; nothing on this list needs new tooling.

SizeWidth and depth within the range stated per model; frame height fixed or adjustable.
ColourPowder coat matched to a RAL number or to your sample.
ConfigurationDoor type, interior layout and accessory set per model.
BrandingLogo plate, silk-screen or laser mark; own-brand carton and manual.
PackingFlat pack with moisture barrier; mixed-SKU container plan on request.
